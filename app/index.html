    Add a matter

    Enter this time in iTimeKeep · a helper that fills iTK's entry form for you
    1. One-time setup: drag this button to your Edge favorites bar: TL → iTimeKeep
      Can't drag it? Click , then right-click the favorites bar → Add page, name it "TL → iTimeKeep", and paste into the URL box.
    2. Choose the dates above and click Copy for iTimeKeep.
    3. Open iTimeKeep in Edge and click the TL → iTimeKeep favorite. Paste into the panel that appears.
    4. The first time, the helper watches you start one practice entry: open the entry window, look up a matter (open the list, search, pick it), and click the Hours and Narrative fields. Then close that entry without saving.
    5. For each entry, click Fill in iTimeKeep, check the entry, and click iTK's own Save. The helper never saves for you.

    Each matter needs its iTK matter number (Matters tab). Your entries go to iTimeKeep only through your clipboard and your own clicks; nothing is sent anywhere else.
